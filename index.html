Classes and school years

Years 1–7 use house names (Ivrella, Emilora, Johansen, Caspiella, Everett). Years 8–10 use profile names: General Stuff, Spells, Hand Magic, and Important Magic.

Subjects, teachers and year limits

Create each subject once. Add all the teachers who can teach it. Teacher names must be unique across every subject. Teacher counts are kept to the minimum needed if each teacher takes 25 lessons a week. Then set how many lessons per week each year or profile group is allowed to have. Years 1–5 share one limit; Year 6 and Year 7 are separate; Years 8–10 use profile limits (General Stuff 8/10, Spells 8/10, etc.).

Example: Potions can be 3 for Years 1–5 and 6 for General Stuff 8/10. Every Years 1–5 class can have up to 3 Potions lessons per week, while General Stuff classes in Years 8–10 can have up to 6.
