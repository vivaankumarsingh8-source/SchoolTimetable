Subjects, teachers and year limits

Create each subject once. Add all the teachers who can teach it. Teacher names must be unique across every subject. Then set how many lessons per week each year is allowed to have. These limits apply separately to each class in that year.

Example: Mathematics could have a limit of 4 lessons in Year 1 and 6 lessons in Year 10. Every Year 1 class can have up to 4 Mathematics lessons per week, while every Year 10 class can have up to 6.
